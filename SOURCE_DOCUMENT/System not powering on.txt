Issue : System not powering on
1.First, make sure the power cord is securely plugged into an electrical outlet. 
2.If the system still does not turn on, check to ensure that the power supply is properly seated in its slot and secured with a screw or clip. 
3.It's also possible that a fuse may have blown within the power supply; if so, replace it with a fuse of the same amperage rating. 
4.Finally, try resetting the motherboard by removing all connected components (except for the power supply and memory), disconnecting the battery, waiting for 30 seconds, reinstalling the battery, and reattaching the components.
5.If none of these steps work, it's possible that a component, such as the motherboard or CPU, has failed and will need to be replaced.
